Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FitHub is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account (username and email), place an order (delivery details), or subscribe to a plan. Payment card details are handled securely by Stripe and are never stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders and subscriptions, send order confirmations, manage your account, and improve our services.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted service providers (such as Stripe for payments) as needed to operate the service.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to maintain your shopping cart. These are necessary for the website to function.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You can also manage your account details directly.
